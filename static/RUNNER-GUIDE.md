# Runner Tracking — Quick Start Guide

## How to Track Your Race Position Live

You'll be able to see your position on the race map in real-time. Here's how:

---

## 📱 OPTION 1: Install the App (Recommended — Background Tracking)

**Works on Android and iPhone**

### Step 1: Download the App

**Android:**
- Click this link to download: [app-debug.apk](app-debug.apk)
- Tap to install
- Allow "unknown sources" if prompted

**iPhone:**
- Ask the race organizer for the TestFlight link
- Or use Option 2 below (browser tracker)

### Step 2: Set Up Your Tracker (Two Ways)

#### Method A: Scan the QR Code (Fastest)
1. Go to the race control room
2. Open **Runner Tracker** app on your phone
3. Scan the **QR code** displayed at the control station
4. Your bib number and server URL will be filled in automatically
5. Tap **"START TRACKING"**

#### Method B: Enter Manually
1. Open **Runner Tracker**
2. Enter your **bib number** (e.g., 142)
3. Enter the **server URL**: `https://[your-tunnel-url].trycloudflare.com`
4. Tap **"START TRACKING"**

### Step 3: Allow Permissions

1. When asked, allow **Location permission**
2. Choose **"Allow while using app"** (iPhone: choose **"Always"**)
3. If asked about battery optimization, choose **"Allow"**

### Step 4: Put Your Phone Away

- **Keep the app running** in the background
- **Your phone can be in your pocket** with the screen off
- The app will keep sending your GPS location every 20 seconds
- Your position will appear on the race map automatically

---

## 🌐 OPTION 2: Use the Browser (No App Needed)

**Works on any phone with a web browser**

### Step 1: Open the URL

1. Open Safari or Chrome on your phone
2. Go to: `https://[your-tunnel-url].trycloudflare.com/tracker`

### Step 2: Enter Your Details

1. Enter your **bib number**
2. Tap **"START TRACKING"**
3. Allow location permission when prompted

### Step 3: Keep Your Screen On

- **You must keep the app open** with the screen on
- **Do NOT put your phone in your pocket** — tracking stops when the screen is off
- Keep your phone in hand or a front pocket where you can see the screen

---

## Race Day Checklist

- [ ] You know the server URL
- [ ] You allowed location permission

## Troubleshooting

**GPS stops after a few minutes?**
- Go to Settings → Runner Tracker → Battery → Select "Unrestricted"

**App crashes on startup?**
- Check the server URL — it must start with `https://`

**Marker doesn't appear on the map?**
- Wait 20 seconds (pings happen every 20 seconds)
- Make sure you're outside (GPS needs sky view)
- Check the server URL is correct

**Runner can't install the app?**
- Use Option 2 (browser tracker) instead
